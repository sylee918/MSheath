FORMAT OF THE SUBSETTED TRAINING/VALIDATION FILES
    
##   ITEMS                         FORMAT   
     
 1   Year                                       I4
 2   Day Of Year (Jan 1 = DOY 1)                I4
 3   Hour of Day (0-23)                         I3
 4   Minute of Hour (0-59)                      I3
 5   S/C XGSM position (in Re=6371.2km)         F10.5
 6   S/C YGSM position                          F10.5
 7   S/C ZGSM position                          F10.5
 8   observed BXGSM (nT)                        F8.1
 9   observed BYGSM (nT)                        F8.1
10   observed BZGSM (nT)                        F8.1
11   observed proton density (cm^-3)            F6.2
12   observed proton energy (eV)                F7.1
13   bulk plasma VXGSE (km/s)                   F8.1    
14   bulk plasma VYGSE (km/s)                   F8.1    
15   bulk plasma VZGSE (km/s)                   F8.1    
16   IMF BXGSM (nT)                             F8.2
17   IMF BYGSM (nT)                             F8.2
18   IMF BZGSM (nT)                             F8.2
19   Solar wind VXGSE (km/s)                    F8.1    
20   Solar wind VYGSE (km/s; 4-deg aberrated)   F8.1    
21   Solar wind VZGSE (km/s)                    F8.1    
22   Solar wind proton density (cm^-3)          F7.2    
23   Solar wind ram pressure (nPa)              F6.2    
24   Solar wind proton energy (eV)              F7.1    
25   Solar wind Mach Alfven number              F6.1    
26   Solar wind magnetosonic Mach number        F5.1    
27   Spacecraft ID                              A3      
28   Record association (either MSH or MSP)     A4  
     such that ' MSH' or ' MSP' stand for magnetosheath or magnetosphere, resp.

FORMAT STATEMENT: 

FORMAT(2I4,2I3,2x,3f10.5,3f8.1,2x,f6.2,f7.1,3f8.1,2x,3f8.2,3F8.1,f7.2,f7.1,f6.2,f6.1,f5.1,2X,A3,A4)
